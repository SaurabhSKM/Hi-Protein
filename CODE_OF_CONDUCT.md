# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Be ruthless about numbers.

## What we expect

Everyone taking part in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions — is expected
to behave professionally and courteously. Concretely:

- Assume the person on the other side is competent and acting in good faith.
- Criticise the work, not the person. "This figure double-counts the eco-scheme uplift"
  is useful. "You clearly don't understand CAP" is not, and is usually also wrong.
- Welcome questions from people new to agronomy, to French open data, or to the code.
  Nobody arrives knowing what BCAE 8 is.
- Accept a correction gracefully. Several of this project's most important changes came
  from someone pointing out that a number was flattering.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sustained disruption, publishing
others' private information, or unwelcome attention of any kind. This applies to public
and private project channels alike.

## On disagreement about the numbers

Challenging a figure, a parameter or a modelling choice is **encouraged**, and doing so
forcefully is fine. This project reports money figures to farmers and area figures to
policy readers; a plausible-looking wrong number is its most expensive failure mode. Use
the [data or method issue template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/data_or_method.yml) and bring
evidence.

The line is simple: argue about the data as hard as you like, and treat the person
holding it with respect.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers, privately. Reports will be handled
confidentially and in proportion. Maintainers who do not uphold this document can expect
to be held to it themselves.
